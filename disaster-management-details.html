AI-Based Disaster Response Management System

AI-powered disaster resource management platform for resource demand prediction, disaster impact assessment, logistics optimization, mission coordination, and relief operations.

Project Overview

During disaster events, resource allocation decisions for food, water, medical supplies, search and rescue teams, and shelter are often made with incomplete information. This project develops a full-stack AI-powered disaster resource management platform that integrates disaster event information, geospatial data, population information, and field resource inventory to support disaster impact assessment, resource demand prediction, and optimized relief operations.


The platform provides a centralized command environment where relief coordinators can manage disasters, resources, vehicles, field teams, missions, SOS requests, logistics, and AI-based resource predictions. Field teams can access mission assignments and location-based disaster information through a responsive interface.

Objective

The main objective is to improve disaster response coordination by using AI-based demand prediction, geospatial visualization, resource tracking, and logistics planning to support faster and more informed relief operations.

Core Modules

01

Disaster Data & Impact Assessment

Disaster information is collected and visualized with geographic locations, severity information, affected zones, and map-based impact assessment.

02

AI Resource Demand Prediction

The trained machine learning model predicts demand for food, water, medical kits, and shelter based on disaster-zone characteristics and population-related information.

03

Logistics & Resource Management

Resources and vehicles are managed through the platform while logistics plans support allocation, routing, resource distribution, and relief execution.

04

Missions & Field Operations

Field teams receive mission assignments and can view related disaster locations, vehicles, and operational information using location-based mapping.

AI Resource Demand Prediction

Gradient Boosting Model

The system uses a trained Gradient Boosting based regression model to estimate resource requirements for disaster-affected zones.

Multi-Resource Prediction

The prediction engine estimates requirements for food, water, medical kits, and shelter.

Vulnerability-Based Adjustment

Zone vulnerability information is considered while calculating resource requirements so that areas with higher vulnerable populations can receive appropriate demand adjustments.

Scenario Simulation

The platform also provides scenario simulation to evaluate resource demand based on different input conditions.

System Workflow

1

Disaster Data
Disaster information and geographic locations are collected and stored in the system.

2

Impact & Zone Assessment
Affected zones are identified using location, population, severity, and vulnerability information.

3

AI Demand Prediction
The trained machine learning model predicts required food, water, medical, and shelter resources.

4

Resource & Vehicle Allocation
Available resources and vehicles are considered for disaster response operations.

5

Logistics Optimization
The system generates resource allocation and logistics plans based on demand and available resources.

6

Mission Execution
Field teams receive missions and operational information required for relief activities.

7

Monitoring & Coordination
Coordinators monitor disasters, resources, missions, vehicles, SOS requests, and logistics through the dashboard.

Platform Capabilities

The platform provides a centralized disaster operations dashboard where administrators and emergency coordinators can monitor disasters, manage resources, vehicles, field teams, missions, SOS requests, and logistics. Field teams receive mobile-friendly operational views with location-based mission information.


The system connects the AI prediction layer with operational resource and logistics modules, allowing predicted demand to support practical disaster-response decisions.
