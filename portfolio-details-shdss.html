Smart Health & Driving Safety System

AI, IoT, and Machine Learning-powered system for real-time driving safety

Project Overview

Road accidents, often caused by fatigue, drunk driving, and poor health, require proactive safety measures. The Smart Health & Driving Safety System (SHDSS) enhances road safety through real-time health monitoring, fatigue detection, and intelligent vehicle control.

The system includes Automatic Emergency Braking (AEB), drowsiness detection, alcohol monitoring, and adaptive speed control for adverse conditions.

It also integrates renewable energy sources such as solar and wind power for vehicle lighting, radio, and AC ventilation, supporting safer and sustainable driving.

Key Features

Health Monitoring

Monitors driver health conditions in real time using integrated sensors.

Drowsiness Detection

Uses eye tracking and machine learning-based verification to detect driver drowsiness and intoxication.

Alcohol Monitoring

Detects alcohol levels and applies safety actions based on the detected level.

Intelligent Vehicle Control

The system can reduce vehicle speed for low health or medium alcohol levels and prevent vehicle startup for high alcohol levels.

Automatic Emergency Braking

Provides Automatic Emergency Braking as part of the driving safety mechanism.

Renewable Energy

Integrates solar and wind energy for vehicle lighting, radio, and AC ventilation.

Safety Control

Low Health Condition

The system reduces vehicle speed to support safer driving.

Medium Alcohol Level

The system slows down the vehicle as a safety intervention.

High Alcohol Level

The system prevents vehicle startup to prioritize drunk driving prevention.

Driver Verification

Eye tracking and machine learning are used to verify the driver's condition.

Technologies Used

Artificial Intelligence IoT Machine Learning Sensor Integration Embedded Systems Microcontroller Solar Power Wind Power

System Components

Embedded System

Microcontroller-based system integrated with the vehicle and seat belt for driver-focused monitoring.

Health Sensors

Sensors monitor driver-related health conditions including heart rate and other safety parameters.

Alcohol Sensor

Detects alcohol levels and provides input to the vehicle safety control mechanism.

Eye Tracking

Tracks the driver's eyes to support drowsiness and intoxication verification.

System Workflow

01. Driver Monitoring

Health, alcohol, and eye-related parameters are monitored continuously.

02. Condition Detection

Sensor and machine learning inputs are used to identify unsafe driving conditions.

03. Driver Verification

Eye tracking supports verification that the monitored condition belongs to the driver.

04. Safety Intervention

The system applies appropriate vehicle control such as speed reduction or startup prevention.

05. Sustainable Power

Solar and wind energy are used to support selected vehicle electrical requirements.

Project Outcome

SHDSS combines AI, IoT, machine learning, sensors, embedded systems, and renewable energy concepts to provide proactive driving safety through real-time monitoring and intelligent vehicle interventions.
